72nd BPSC Mains Answer Writing Strategy Guide

Derived directly from examiner tick-marks, question-wise scores, and evaluated BPSC copies.

The Critical Finding GS-1: 169/300 • GS-2: 143/300

Skipping 38-mark long questions is the #1 mark leak (90–100 marks lost). Competent writing on attempted long answers consistently earns 19–23/38 (>50%), and even a structured 1.5-page answer earns 14–18/38. Never leave a long question blank!

Part 1 • The Examiner Scoring Architecture

Short Answers (7–8 Marks) Observed: 2–8/8 • ~8 Mins

Examiners use a "1 valid point = 1 tick = 1–1.5 marks" model. Write 5–6 distinct, examiner-tickable points (each 2–3 lines of substance) to score 7–8/8. Tight structure alone guarantees 4.5–5.5/8.

Long Answers (38 Marks) Observed: 0–23/38 • ~30 Mins

Scored holistically with point-density awareness. Sketch a 3–4 sub-theme outline in 30 seconds and write 8–10 lines per sub-section. Thin answers score near zero, while 1.5 structured pages earn 14–18/38.

Essay Paper (100 Marks × 2 Attempted) Observed: 54–63/100

Topic selection strategy is half the battle. Attempt the 2 topics where you can generate 5+ multi-dimensional angles + mandatory Bihar governance/culture examples within 3 minutes.

✓ What Examiners Are Rewarding (6 Tick Drivers)

  • 1. Point-Wise Visual Hierarchy: Numbered points 1), 2), 3), underlined subheadings (Social:, Significance →), and arrow connectors (→) earn left-margin ticks.
  • 2. Cause → Event → Significance Structure: Used in Santhal (5/8) & Champaran (4.5/8). Citing exact numbers & leader names ("50,000 tribals from 400 villages") earns dedicated ticks.
  • 3. Quotes with Named Attribution: Quoting S. Jaishankar (MEA) in the India-G20 answer (20/38) earned a direct tick. Use 1–2 named expert/minister/committee quotes per long answer.
  • 4. Explicit Concluding Lines: Ending every answer with "Therefore..." or "Thus..." signals complete, structured thinking.
  • 5. Breadth Across Sub-Themes: Connecting V-20 island nations, Solar Alliance growth, and renewable multilateralism earned separate ticks—breadth beats single-thread depth.
  • 6. Crossing Unused Space: Crossing (X) unused blank space on every page is a mandatory hygiene requirement.

✗ What Examiners Are Penalising (6 Mark Killers)

  • 1. Thin Single-Liner Short Answers: BNSS 2023 (8M) scored only 2/8 despite 6 points because each point was just 1 line. Every point must have 2–3 lines of substance.
  • 2. Skipping Long Questions (Zero Pattern): Leaving 38-mark questions blank is the most catastrophic error when 1.5 structured pages easily earn 14–18/38.
  • 3. Flowing Prose Without Sub-Structure: Paragraph-style short answers prevent individual point ticking and lose 1.5–2 marks per 8-mark question.
  • 4. Missing the "Critical" Dimension: In "Critically describe the amendment process" (scored 4/8), omitting criticism (rigidity, simple majority misuse, Basic Structure Doctrine) caps marks at ~50%.
  • 5. No Bihar-Specific Governance Content: Staying only at the national level without citing SFC, Bihar Panchayati Raj Act, Jeevika, or Bihar Economic Survey 2025-26 keeps scores average.
  • 6. Writing in the Margin: Any content written in the designated margin is ignored by the evaluator.
GS Paper I • History, Culture & Geography

✓ DO: Use the 3-layer History structure: Colonial Context → Event/Movement → Significance/Legacy. Name leaders, participant counts & districts.

✓ DO: Use arrow timelines: 1905 → Partition of Bengal → 1906 → Muslim League → 1907 → Surat Split.

✗ DON'T: Don't stop after listing causes in a revolt question—omitting the Significance section loses 2 full marks.

GS Paper II • Polity, Governance & Social Issues

✓ DO: Include (a) Article/Amendment, (b) Landmark SC Case, and (c) Practical implementation example. Use Problem → Policy → Implementation Gap → Way Forward.

✓ DO: Cite NFHS data, NITI Aayog reports, SFC recommendations, Bihar Panchayati Raj Act, ATMA, & Jeevika.

✗ DON'T: Never end with generic lines like "Govt should take steps." Conclude with named Bihar/national policy schemes.

Economy, Science & Tech & Environment

✓ DO (Economy): Follow Concept → Current Status (Data) → Bihar Angle → Challenges → Way Forward with Bihar Economic Survey 2025-26 & Union Budget 2025-26 figures.

✓ DO (S&T / Env): 1-line definition → 4–5 real-world applications (1 tick each) → ISRO/DRDO/India relevance → Bihar Ganga plains, Kosi floods, Farakka Barrage & SAPCC.

✗ DON'T: Avoid vague claims like "GDP growth is low." Write specific numbers from the Bihar Economic Survey.

Essay Paper (100 Marks × 2 Essays)

✓ DO: Open with a striking quote, paradox, or news hook. Use a 5-paragraph minimum framework with subheadings: Intro → Economic/Political → Social/Historical → Bihar/India Governance (e-Shasan Bihar, Voter Helpline, BPDA) → 10-Year Vision Conclusion.

✗ DON'T: Don't write purely literary/philosophical essays lacking policy depth—BPSC evaluators are bureaucracy-track examiners.

Core 72nd Mains Priority: Attempt Every Question • Structure Every Answer • Lead Every Point with Data
